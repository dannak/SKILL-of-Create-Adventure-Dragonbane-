## Stated Goal

Break the barrow's hold on Graymere Ford — free Torben, protect Marta, and put the dead back to rest before the wight claims more lives.

### Scene 2 — The Elder's Story

**Chapter:** 2
**Type:** Social / Exploration

**Prose description:**
The village of Graymere Ford is eight low buildings around a muddy common. The flood-line is still visible on the walls — a dark stain two feet up. Agna, the eldest here by two decades, meets the party in the longhouse, hands folded around a clay cup she does not drink from. The other villagers watch from doorways.

**GM beats:**
- Agna explains: the barrow has been there longer than the village. Her grandmother struck a pact — offerings of grain and silver left at the mound's entrance each new moon keep the dead quiet.
- The spring flood cracked the stone slab sealing the entrance. She sent Torben in yesterday to check the seal. He hasn't come back.
- Marta started sleepwalking toward the mound the same night.
- Agna does not volunteer her secret unless pressed (Persuasion or Insight roll, difficulty 14): she missed three consecutive offerings during a hard winter two years ago and has been doubling up ever since, hoping it was enough.

**NPC presence:**
- Agna — protect the village — missed offerings may have weakened the seal before the flood even hit (secret)
- Liss — keep Marta inside and safe — will beg the party to get Torben out too

**Mechanical needs:**
- Insight (difficulty 14) to notice Agna is holding something back
- Persuasion (difficulty 12) to get her to admit the missed offerings
- Lore (difficulty 10) to recall general knowledge about barrow-pacts in Ravenland

**Encounter reference:** None

**Fail-forward:** If the party skips talking to Agna and goes straight to the barrow, they miss the mound layout (harder navigation in Scene 3) and don't know about Torben — they find him confused, with no context for why he's there.

### Scene 2b — Torben's Cottage (Sidetrack)

**Chapter:** 2
**Type:** Exploration
**Rejoin point:** Scene 3 — the party arrives at the barrow entrance with additional knowledge.

**Prose description:**
Torben's cottage is the farthest from the common, half-swallowed by the treeline. Inside: a shepherd's mess, a straw bed, and on the table a folded piece of cured leather covered in careful scratches — a rough map of the mound's interior, labeled in an old hand. A note in fresher ink: *Three chambers. Seal urn in the deep room. Don't let the light go out.*

**GM beats:**
- The leather map shows three chambers: an entry passage, a middle room with a pit hazard, and the inner hall where the urn rests.
- The note "don't let the light go out" is a practical warning — the wight can extinguish mundane flames at will.
- A small pouch on the shelf holds three silver coins and a stub of tallow candle — Torben's prepared supplies that he apparently left behind.

**NPC presence:** None (Torben is already in the mound).

**Mechanical needs:**
- Awareness (difficulty 10) to find the map under a folded blanket
- The silver coins count as improvised weapons vs. undead (treat as +2 damage)

**Encounter reference:** None

**Fail-forward:** If the party skips this scene, they enter the barrow without the map. The pit hazard in Scene 3 requires an Awareness roll (difficulty 14) to spot rather than being automatically visible.

### Scene 3 — The Outer Chambers

**Chapter:** 3
**Type:** Exploration / Tension

**Prose description:**
The mound's entrance is a low stone arch, the sealing slab cracked in two by the flood and shoved inward. Cold air breathes out. The passage beyond is shoulder-width, walls sweating moisture, and ahead the darkness is absolute. Something shuffles in the middle distance — footsteps, too slow and too even to be a frightened man's.

**GM beats:**
- Entry passage (30 ft): narrow, damp, no threats — but mundane torches gutter and reduce visibility radius by half.
- Middle chamber: a pit (10 ft wide, 15 ft deep, spiked with old stakes) crosses the full width. A stone ledge on the left side is 18 inches wide — traversable but nerve-wracking.
  - With the map: automatically visible; AGL roll difficulty 10 to cross.
  - Without the map: Awareness difficulty 14 to spot before stepping; AGL roll difficulty 12 to cross.
  - Falling: 2d6 damage; the party can hear Torben's shuffling stop when they fall.
- Beyond the pit: Torben. He stands in the passage, eyes open and empty, blocking the way to the inner hall. He does not attack but will not step aside; he moves to physically obstruct whoever tries to pass.
  - Grapple or Persuasion (difficulty 14, shouting his name, something personal) to break the wight's hold long enough to get past. Success: Torben collapses, confused but free. Failure: the party must restrain him — costs an action each round until they pass.

**NPC presence:**
- Torben — guard the inner hall — is being controlled by the wight; his secret is irrelevant here, he's not himself

**Mechanical needs:**
- Torben grapple: opposed STR roll; Torben's STR 11
- Awareness to spot pit (without map): difficulty 14
- AGL to cross ledge: difficulty 10 (with map) or 12 (without)

**Encounter reference:** None (Torben does not deal damage unless the party attacks him first)

**Fail-forward:** If Torben is knocked unconscious trying to stop the party, he lies in the passage — alive, and recovers after the climax. If the party falls in the pit, they take damage and must climb out (STR difficulty 12, or help from a rope); the wight does not pursue them into the pit.

### Scene 4 — The Wight's Hall (Climax)

**Chapter:** 3
**Type:** Combat / Resolution

**Prose description:**
The inner hall is wider than the passage — enough to breathe. At the far end, on a raised stone bier, rests a clay urn the size of a man's torso, cracked clean across the middle, its two halves held together by dried clay daubed in old rune-marks, now split open. Before it stands a figure in the armor of a century long past, its face a hollow where a face should be. When it speaks, it speaks in a voice like grinding stone: *"The pact is broken. The debt comes due."*

**GM beats:**
- The Barrow Wight can be resolved two ways:
  - **Restore the seal:** The party notices (Lore difficulty 12, or automatically if they asked Agna about the pact) that the urn's crack can be re-sealed with the silver from Torben's pouch or any silver the party carries, packed into the crack and the rune-marks traced with a blade. Takes two full rounds of uninterrupted action. The wight will try to stop them.
  - **Defeat in combat:** see Encounter 2 in encounters.md. Harder path — the wight is a genuine threat at starting advancement.
- If the urn is re-sealed mid-combat: the wight freezes, utters "The debt is… held," and retreats into the bier. Combat ends.
- If the wight is defeated: the urn's power collapses entirely — the barrow is permanently empty. The village is safe but loses the pact's protection forever (GM note for future sessions).
- Torben, if freed in Scene 3, stirs and stumbles in during the climax — too weak to fight but can hold a torch or hand over the silver coins.

**Stated goal payoff:** The wight's opening declaration ("the debt comes due") directly mirrors the opening — the party learned in Scene 2 that a pact was broken. They now face the consequence and have two ways to honor the goal: restore the pact, or end it.

**NPC presence:**
- The Barrow Wight — enforce the broken pact — does not know (or care) that Agna, not the flood, first weakened the seal

**Mechanical needs:**
- Sealing the urn: two uninterrupted actions (Crafts or Lore difficulty 10 each); silver required
- Wight combat: see Encounter 2
- Torben's silver coins: +2 damage vs. the wight if used as improvised weapon (thrown or pressed into a strike)

**Encounter reference:** Encounter 2 — The Barrow Wight

**Fail-forward:** If the party is defeated: the wight does not kill them. It takes what it considers its due — one item of value from each character (the "debt" paid in goods) — and retreats to the bier. The party wakes outside the mound at dawn, alive. The wight's hold on Marta fades. The barrow remains active but dormant; the pact is not restored. Agna's guilt is now a live thread.
