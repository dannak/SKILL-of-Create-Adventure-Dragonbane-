# Encounters — The Barrow at Graymere Ford

---

## Encounter 1: The Risen at the Ford

**Scene:** Scene 1 (Opening)
**Type:** Open combat
**Trigger:** Party approaches the ford and sees Liss dragging Marta away from the barrow.

**Base monster:** Risen (Draugr-type undead)
**Source:** General knowledge — no reference file.

**Scaling inputs:**
- Party: 3 × Starting (bracket 1)
- Difficulty: Standard
- Base stats (general knowledge): HP 8, STR 12, AGL 8, INT 5, WIL 8, CHA 3, Ferocity 2
- Base damage: Claws 1d6, Bite 1d6+2

**Adjustments applied:**
- Difficulty modifier: Standard — no stat change.
- Party size adjustment: 3 players (below default 4) — reduce HP by 25%: 8 → 6 each.
- Monster count: 2 (standard for an opening encounter; manageable for 3 starting characters).
- Swap applied: No.

**Final roster:**

| Monster | Count | HP | STR | AGL | INT | WIL | CHA | Ferocity | Armor | Damage |
|---------|-------|----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|----------|-------|--------|
| Risen   | 2     | 6  | 12  | 8   | 5   | 8   | 3   | 2        | 0     | Claws 1d6 / Bite 1d6+2 |

**Special abilities:**
- **Unnatural Strength:** Once per combat, one Risen adds +4 STR to a single attack roll.
- **Undead:** Immune to Scared condition. Do not check morale.
- **Vulnerability — Fire:** Fire damage deals +2 per die.

**Expected resource cost:**
- WP: ~4–6 (low; Ferocity 2 means few special ability triggers)
- HP: ~3–6 (one or two hits landing across the party)
- Conditions: Low risk — Ferocity 2 Risen do not impose conditions
- Session budget share: ~10% WP / 5% HP

**Tactics:**
The Risen move directly toward Marta, not toward the party — they are herding her back to the mound. They will engage the party only if physically blocked. One targets Marta's path; the other intercepts the party. They do not retreat, do not speak, and use Unnatural Strength only when within reach of Marta (to shove past a blocker). They fight until destroyed.

**Terrain:**
- Ford: Difficult Ground (half Movement, AGL difficulty 12 to move without slipping).
- Near bank: firm mud, no penalties.
- Far bank: steep 3-ft rise; Climb required (STR difficulty 8) if approaching from the water.

**Defeat consequence:**
The Risen do not kill unconscious characters. If the party is downed, the Risen resume herding Marta toward the barrow. Liss screams; village men with torches come running within 1 round and drive the Risen back with fire (they retreat). The party wakes on the near bank. Marta is shaken but physically unharmed.

**Victory consequence:**
When both Risen are destroyed, Marta blinks awake as if from a dream. Liss collapses in relief. The village watches from the far bank. Agna is already waiting.

## Encounter 2: The Barrow Wight

**Scene:** Scene 4 (Climax)
**Type:** Combat / Resolution (two valid paths — seal or fight)
**Trigger:** The party enters the inner hall.

**Base monster:** Barrow Wight (ancient guardian)
**Source:** General knowledge — no reference file.

**Scaling inputs:**
- Party: 3 × Starting (bracket 1)
- Difficulty: Standard
- Base stats (general knowledge): HP 20, STR 16, AGL 10, INT 12, WIL 14, CHA 8, Ferocity 3
- Base damage: Touch of the Grave — 1d8 WIL drain (not HP; targets Willpower Points directly)

**Adjustments applied:**
- Difficulty modifier: Standard — no change to stats.
- Party size adjustment: 3 players — reduce HP by 25%: 20 → 15.
- WIL drain damage: reduce die from 1d8 to 1d6 for party size.
- Swap applied: No.

**Final roster:**

| Monster | Count | HP | STR | AGL | INT | WIL | CHA | Ferocity | Armor | Damage |
|---------|-------|----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|----------|-------|--------|
| Barrow Wight | 1 | 15 | 16 | 10 | 12 | 14 | 8 | 3 | 2 (spectral plate) | Touch 1d6 WP / Slam 1d8 HP |

**Special abilities:**
- **Touch of the Grave:** Melee attack that drains 1d6 WP from the target instead of dealing HP damage. A character reduced to 0 WP by this ability gains the Exhausted condition immediately.
- **Dread Aura:** At the start of combat, all characters within 10m must make a WIL roll (difficulty 12) or gain the Scared condition for the rest of the scene. Characters with Scared condition take −2 to all rolls.
- **Extinguish:** Once per round as a free action, the wight can snuff any mundane light source within 20m. Magical or silver light is unaffected. Torben's warning ("don't let the light go out") refers to this — fighting in darkness imposes −4 to all attack rolls.
- **Vulnerability — Silver:** Silver weapons or silver used as an improvised weapon deal +2 damage per die. Torben's silver coins count.

**Expected resource cost:**
- WP: ~12–18 (Ferocity 3, repeated Touch of the Grave; the primary threat vector)
- HP: ~6–10 (Slam used when party is bunched or a character is prone)
- Conditions: High — Dread Aura likely applies Scared to 1–2 characters; 1+ characters risk Exhausted from WP drain
- Session budget share: ~35% WP / 15% HP (climax encounter; designed to be the session's main expenditure)

**Tactics:**
The wight opens with Dread Aura the moment characters enter the hall — it does not wait. First round: moves toward the character closest to the urn (to block resealing). Subsequent rounds: alternates Touch of the Grave (vs. highest WIL character) and Slam (vs. whoever is between it and the urn). Uses Extinguish on round 2 if the party has only mundane light. It ignores characters who are down; it is not trying to kill, it is trying to deter.

If a character begins the resealing action on the urn, the wight immediately breaks off from all other targets and focuses entirely on interrupting that character.

**Terrain:**
- Inner hall: 20 ft wide, 30 ft long. Stone bier at far end. Low ceiling (6 ft) — no penalty for human-scale characters.
- The cracked urn sits on the bier. It requires a character to be adjacent to the bier and spend two uninterrupted actions (Crafts or Lore difficulty 10 each) to reseal.
- The hall has no exits besides the passage back — no flanking routes.

**Defeat consequence:**
The wight does not kill unconscious characters. If all party members are downed or flee: it takes one valued item from each downed character (the "debt paid in goods") and returns to the bier. The party wakes outside at dawn. Marta's pull fades. The barrow is dormant but not sealed — the pact is unpaid and still broken. This is a live thread for future sessions.

**Victory consequence — Seal restored:**
The wight says "The debt is held" and withdraws into the bier. The bier seals shut. The barrow returns to dormancy. The pact is active again. Agna will maintain offerings properly now. Torben takes credit for knowing how; will cheerfully accept corrections.

**Victory consequence — Wight destroyed:**
The urn collapses entirely. The barrow empties — any remaining Risen crumble. The village is permanently free of the pact but also permanently unprotected. The wight's destruction frees something ancient; GM note: the mound may attract other things in future sessions now that the guardian is gone.
