# Handouts — The Barrow at Graymere Ford

---

### Handout 1: Torben's Note

**Type:** Found document
**Obtained:** Scene 2b — Torben's Cottage (on the table beside the leather map)
**Relevance:** Warns about the wight's ability to extinguish light; implies silver is required for resealing; creates urgency (Torben went in knowing the danger).

**Text:**

> *Three chambers. Seal urn in the deep room. Don't let the light go out.*
>
> *Silver in the crack, trace the marks with iron. Hold until it sets. Grandmother said it takes two pairs of hands.*
>
> *— T*

---

### Handout 2: The Mound Map

**Type:** Found document (sketch on cured leather)
**Obtained:** Scene 2b — Torben's Cottage (folded under a blanket near the table)
**Relevance:** Shows the three-chamber layout; reveals the pit hazard in the middle room; allows the party to cross safely with a lower difficulty roll.

**Text:**

> *[A rough sketch on aged leather, the lines scratched with a knife. Three connected shapes: a long entry passage labeled "wet — watch footing"; a wider middle room with a dark gap across the middle labeled "pit — left ledge only"; and the inner hall labeled "deep room — urn on the stone." In the margin, an old hand has written "speak no names of the living." Below it, in fresh ink: "I don't know what that means either."]*

*(Read the description aloud; no physical prop needed unless you want to draw it.)*

---

### Handout 3: The Wight's Words

**Type:** NPC monologue (read-aloud when the wight speaks)
**Obtained:** Scene 4 — spoken when the party enters the inner hall
**Relevance:** States the wight's position clearly; signals that a bargain (resealing) is possible.

**Text:**

> *"The pact is broken. The debt comes due."*
>
> *(If the party holds ground and does not immediately attack:)*
>
> *"You stand in the house of the dead. You carry silver. You know the marks. Then you know what must be done. Do it, and I return to my sleep. Fail, and I take what the pact is owed."*
