# NPCs — The Barrow at Graymere Ford

---

### Agna

**Role:** Neutral / Key information source
**Affiliation:** Graymere Ford village (elder, de facto leader)
**Appears in:** Scene 2

**Stat line:** Not a combatant.
HP: 8  STR: 9  AGL: 8  INT: 15  WIL: 14  CHA: 13

**Immediate goal:** Keep the party focused on solving the barrow problem quickly, before anyone else is taken.
**Long-term goal:** Protect the village she has led for thirty years.
**Secret:** She missed three consecutive new-moon offerings two winters ago during a hard season. She has been doubling up ever since and telling herself it was fine. She believes — probably correctly — that this weakened the seal before the flood ever touched it, making her partially responsible for everything that followed.
**Motive:** Shame and love for her people in equal measure. She withholds the secret because admitting it means admitting she put everyone at risk for the sake of a few handfuls of grain.

**Voice & mannerism:**
Speaks in short declarative sentences; does not ask questions she doesn't already know the answer to. Keeps her hands folded or busy — never idle. When she is lying or withholding, she pours more into a cup that is already full.

**How they change:** If the party uncovers or she confesses the missed offerings, she becomes fully cooperative — gives them everything she knows, including details about the urn and the rune-marks that can reseal it. Relief at being caught is visible and immediate.

### Liss

**Role:** Ally (emotional anchor)
**Affiliation:** Graymere Ford village
**Appears in:** Scene 1, Scene 2

**Stat line:** Not a combatant.

**Immediate goal:** Keep her daughter Marta inside and safe.
**Long-term goal:** Understand what has hold of her child and make it stop.
**Secret:** She has been sneaking extra offerings to the mound on her own, beyond Agna's schedule — small things, a brooch that was her mother's. She doesn't know it doesn't help.
**Motive:** She is terrified and has been since the flood. The extra offerings are the only thing she can do.

**Voice & mannerism:**
Speaks quickly, repeats herself when frightened. Grabs sleeves. Will follow the party to the barrow if not firmly told to stay — she needs to be given a specific task ("guard Marta, do not let her near the water") or she will hover uselessly.

**How they change:** Once the wight's hold on Marta breaks, Liss becomes calm almost instantly — a practical woman who was holding herself together by sheer will. Offers the party a meal, means it.

### Marta

**Role:** Victim / Indicator
**Affiliation:** Graymere Ford village
**Appears in:** Scene 1, Scene 2 (background), Scene 4 (released)

**Stat line:** Child; not a combatant; 6 HP.

**Immediate goal:** (While entranced) reach the barrow. (After breaking) understand what happened to her.
**Long-term goal:** Normal childhood.
**Secret:** She remembers the wight's voice. It told her to come and witness. She has not told her mother because the voice didn't feel frightening when she heard it — it felt like being called home.
**Motive:** The wight's pull is not malice toward Marta specifically — it is using her as leverage over the village.

**Voice & mannerism:**
While entranced: silent, blank, walks steadily. After breaking: confused, clingy, asks questions in bursts. Older than she looks emotionally — she's used to the adults being frightened.

### Torben

**Role:** Ally (in distress)
**Affiliation:** Graymere Ford village
**Appears in:** Scene 3, Scene 4 (if freed)

**Stat line:** STR: 11  AGL: 9  INT: 12  WIL: 10  CHA: 9
HP: 10  Armor: 0  Weapon: Shepherd's crook (1d6)
*(Stats relevant only if the party attempts to restrain him in Scene 3.)*

**Immediate goal:** (While controlled) bar the way to the inner hall. (After freeing) apologize and help.
**Long-term goal:** Fix what he walked in to fix — he still wants to reseal the urn.
**Secret:** He knows exactly how to reseal the urn — his grandmother wrote it down and he has it memorized — but he went in alone because he didn't want to frighten the village by admitting he knew that much about the old pact.
**Motive:** Stubborn competence and misplaced protectiveness.

**Voice & mannerism:**
Normally: dry, laconic, answers questions with fewer words than were asked. Immediately after being freed in Scene 3: disoriented, sits down hard, holds his head. Within two minutes: "Right. The urn. I know how." Stands up. Ready.

**How they change:** If freed, Torben becomes the party's most useful asset in Scene 4 — he can talk them through the resealing process without any roll required, and the silver coins in his cottage were his deliberately.

### The Barrow Wight

**Role:** Antagonist
**Affiliation:** The Dead of the Barrow
**Appears in:** Scene 4

**Stat line:** See encounters.md — Encounter 2.

**Immediate goal:** Collect the debt it is owed under the broken pact — lives or goods, one or the other.
**Long-term goal:** Return to its vigil. It does not want to be awake.
**Secret:** It knows Agna broke the pact before the flood. It knows exactly who is responsible. It has not come for her specifically only because the pact did not specify individuals — it specified the village.
**Motive:** The wight is not evil by choice. It is a guardian bound to its function by an oath sworn in life and reinforced by ritual. The pact broken, it follows the only logic it has. It will accept the debt repaid; it will accept being destroyed; it will not accept being ignored.

**Voice & mannerism:**
Speaks rarely. Each word is deliberate and final. Does not threaten — states. "The pact is broken." "The debt comes due." "You are in the way." If the party attempts to negotiate (Persuasion difficulty 16), it will pause and listen — it respects the form of a bargain, if not the living who make them.

**How they change:** If the urn is resealed, the wight pauses mid-action, says "The debt is held," and withdraws. It does not thank the party. It does not acknowledge them again.
