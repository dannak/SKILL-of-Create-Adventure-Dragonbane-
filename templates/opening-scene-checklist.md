# Opening Scene Checklist

Every opening scene must pass **all four gates** before the skill advances to
scene expansion (Step 6). For each gate, cite the specific beat or sentence
in the hook that satisfies it.

---

## Gate 1 — Action Already in Progress

**Check:** The scene begins mid-action. The PCs are already doing something
or something is already happening *to* them. There is no "you arrive at the
inn and settle in" preamble.

**Pass:** Opening sentence puts PCs or a threat in motion.
**Fail:** Scene starts at rest, in transit, or with exposition.

*Fix: Rewrite the first sentence to drop the PCs into the middle of a
concrete physical event.*

---

## Gate 2 — Stated Goal

**Check:** Within the first scene, a clear, specific goal is established —
something the PCs can pursue, solve, or decide. This goal must thread through
to the climax.

**Pass:** Goal is named explicitly in the scene text and noted in the
`## Stated Goal` section of `adventure.md`.
**Fail:** The opening is atmospheric but leaves PCs without a direction.

*Fix: Add one NPC line or discovered clue that names the goal in concrete
terms ("find who burned the mill before dawn" not "investigate the fire").*

---

## Gate 3 — Sensory Detail

**Check:** The scene includes at least two distinct sensory details beyond
sight — sound, smell, texture, temperature, taste.

**Pass:** Two or more non-visual sensory details present.
**Fail:** Scene is purely visual.

*Fix: Add one auditory and one olfactory/tactile detail tied to the scene's
core image.*

---

## Gate 4 — Immediate Decision Point

**Check:** Before the first stretch ends, the PCs face a meaningful choice
with at least two visible options that have different consequences. It must
be a real fork, not a false one.

**Pass:** Decision point named with at least two concrete options and their
visible consequences.
**Fail:** Scene is purely reactive (no choice) or the "choice" has only one
reasonable answer.

*Fix: Name the second option explicitly and give it a visible cost or benefit
distinct from the first.*

---

## Reporting Format

When citing the checklist, output:

```
Gate 1 — Action Already in Progress: PASS
  Beat: "The mill's upper floor collapses as the PCs sprint through the door."

Gate 2 — Stated Goal: PASS
  Beat: "Old Maren gasps 'Find my son — they took him into the forest.'"
  Noted in adventure.md §Stated Goal.

Gate 3 — Sensory Detail: PASS
  Auditory: "Timbers groan and crack overhead."
  Olfactory: "Smoke thick with the sweetness of burning grain."

Gate 4 — Immediate Decision Point: PASS
  Option A: Chase the abductors into the forest now (trail goes cold by dawn).
  Option B: Pull Maren clear first (she has information but delays pursuit).
```
