# Adventure Audit Checklist

Run this checklist after every complete draft. Output `PASS` or `FAIL` for
each item with a one-line fix when failing. Write results to `gm-notes.md`
under `## Audit (Draft N)`.

---

## Category 1 — NPC Motives

**1a.** Every named NPC has a stated motive that is consistent across all
scenes in which they appear.

**1b.** No NPC acts against their stated motive without an in-fiction
explanation (revelation, bribe, threat).

**1c.** The antagonist's plan is self-consistent — they pursue the same goal
throughout and their actions make sense given what they know.

---

## Category 2 — Timeline Coherence

**2a.** Events could have occurred in the stated order given travel times,
in-world distances, and NPC capabilities.

**2b.** No scene depends on an NPC being in two places at once.

**2c.** If a clock or deadline exists, the timeline is consistent with it
(PCs can realistically reach the climax before the deadline).

---

## Category 3 — Clue-to-Resolution

**3a.** Every mystery or secret the adventure expects PCs to solve is seeded
with at least two findable clues (one primary, one backup).

**3b.** Each clue points toward the resolution without requiring a leap the
average player group would not make.

**3c.** No clue is gated behind a single mandatory roll with no alternative.

---

## Category 4 — Goal Threading

**4a.** The Stated Goal from the opening scene is still the core driver of
the climax (or a direct consequence of pursuing it).

**4b.** Every chapter contributes to advancing or complicating the Stated Goal.

**4c.** The denouement addresses what happened to the Stated Goal — resolved,
failed, or transformed.

---

## Category 5 — Scene Reachability

**5a.** Every spine scene can be reached from the previous one without
requiring a specific roll result.

**5b.** No spine scene is gated behind a sidetrack (sidetracks are optional
by definition).

**5c.** If a scene requires a skill roll to enter, there is a fail-forward
path that still moves the adventure forward.

---

## Category 6 — Fail-Forward Paths

**6a.** Every skill roll with significant plot consequence has a documented
fail-forward outcome (failure changes the situation, not just blocks progress).

**6b.** Failure at the climax is a valid outcome with a coherent consequence
(loss condition that makes narrative sense).

**6c.** No single failure can permanently strand the party with no path to
the climax.

---

## Category 7 — Spine Integrity

**7a.** No spine scene can be skipped without the following scenes becoming
incoherent (each scene earns its place).

**7b.** The spine has no dead ends — every scene has at least one clear exit.

**7c.** The climax is a direct consequence of the spine, not a random event.

---

## Category 8 — Sidetrack Rejoin

**8a.** Every sidetrack names the specific spine scene it rejoins.

**8b.** The rejoin point is reachable from the sidetrack's resolution
regardless of how the sidetrack ended (success or failure).

**8c.** Completing a sidetrack provides a meaningful benefit (information,
resource, relationship) — it is not purely decorative.

---

## Category 9 — Tone & Limits

**9a.** No scene crosses a Line from the intake (F2). Check each scene
description against the stated lines.

**9b.** Veiled content (intake F3) is handled off-screen. No detailed
depiction appears in scene prose or GM beats.

**9c.** The overall tone of the draft matches the tone dial from intake (D1).

---

## Reporting Format

```
## Audit (Draft 1)

### Category 1 — NPC Motives
1a: PASS
1b: FAIL — Maren refuses to share the map in Scene 3 despite her motive being
    to rescue her son. Fix: add a line noting she fears the PCs will leave
    without her if they have the map.
1c: PASS

### Category 2 — Timeline Coherence
2a: PASS
...

**Summary:** 2 FAIL items. Revise before presenting to GM.
```
