# Intake Questions

Walk through these groups in order. Mark each slot as filled before
advancing to the next group. Required slots must be answered; optional
slots may be skipped (note the default used downstream).

---

## Group A — Party

**A1. Party size** *(required)*
How many player characters?
→ Used for: encounter headcount, encounter budget.

**A2. Kin and professions overview** *(required)*
Briefly: what kin and profession does each PC have?
→ Used for: NPC hooks, skill-check moments, encounter design.

**A3. Advancement tier** *(required)*
Choose one: Apprentice / Journeyman / Veteran / Master.
→ Used for: monster scaling multipliers in `monster-scaling.md`.

---

## Group B — Pacing

**B1. Session length** *(required)*
How long is the session? (hours) OR how many scenes?
→ Used for: scene count, encounter budget total.

**B2. Scene count target** *(derived from B1 if not given)*
Rule of thumb: 1–2 scenes per hour. Default: 4 scenes for a 3-hour session.

---

## Group C — Difficulty

**C1. Difficulty target** *(required)*
Choose one: Easy / Standard / Hard / Punishing.
→ Used for: encounter budget, monster scaling.

| Difficulty | Resource pressure | Monster HP multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | Low — PCs end full | ×0.75 |
| Standard | Moderate — 1–2 significant drains | ×1.0 |
| Hard | High — PCs noticeably worn | ×1.25 |
| Punishing | Severe — TPK possible | ×1.5 |

---

## Group D — Tone & Genre

**D1. Tone dial** *(optional; default: Standard)*
Where does this session sit?
- Gritty / Dark
- Standard (Dragonbane default)
- Swashbuckling / Pulpy
- Comedic / Whimsical

**D2. Genre dial** *(optional; default: mix)*
Primary genre flavour: Dungeon crawl / Wilderness / Urban intrigue /
Political / Horror / Mystery / Heist.

→ Both used for: opening hook tone, NPC voice, encounter flavour.

---

## Group E — Setting & Continuity

**E1. Location** *(optional; default: unspecified wilderness)*
Where does the session take place? (region, settlement, dungeon name, etc.)
→ Used for: environmental encounter modifiers, NPC factions.

**E2. Active factions** *(optional)*
Which factions are present or relevant? What do they want?
→ Used for: NPC motives, sidetrack hooks, audit motive checks.

**E3. Continuity hooks** *(optional)*
Any ongoing threads from prior sessions to weave in?
→ Used for: opening hook options, NPC relationships.

---

## Group F — Themes & Limits

**F1. Themes** *(optional)*
What themes should the adventure explore? (betrayal, loyalty, survival, etc.)
→ Used for: scene tone, NPC arcs, climax shape.

**F2. Lines** *(optional — hard limits, never cross)*
Topics or content that must not appear, even off-screen.

**F3. Veils** *(optional — handle off-screen only)*
Topics that may exist in the fiction but must not be depicted in detail.

→ F2 and F3 are stored and checked in the audit's Tone & Limits category.
