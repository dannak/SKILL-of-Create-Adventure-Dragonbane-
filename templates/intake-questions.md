# Intake Questions

Walk the GM through these groups in order. Mark each slot as filled once
answered. Slots marked **Required** must be filled before the outline is
drafted. Slots marked **Additional** can be skipped (the GM types "skip").

After each answer, note in parentheses how the answer is used downstream so
the skill can re-derive if any input changes.

---

## Group 1 — Party

**Q1. How many players are in the party?**
*(Required. Used by: encounter scaling, encounter budget, NPC interaction design.)*

**Q2. What kin and professions are in the party?**
List what you know; rough descriptions are fine.
*(Required. Used by: encounter design — which monsters are hard counters,
which scenes spotlight which professions; NPC voice notes.)*

**Q3. What is the party's advancement level?**
Options: Starting / Novice / Experienced / Veteran / Legendary.
Or describe in terms of skills: "most at rank 10–14" etc.
*(Required. Used by: monster-scaling.md to set base threat bracket.)*

---

## Group 2 — Pacing

**Q4. How long is the session?**
Approximate hours, or describe as short (2–3 h), standard (3–4 h), long (4–6 h).
*(Required. Used by: scene count target, encounter budget total.)*

**Q5. How many scenes / chapters do you want?**
Accept a number or "let the skill decide" based on session length.
Rule of thumb: 2–3 scenes per hour for standard pacing.
*(Required. Used by: outline structure, encounter budget distribution.)*

---

## Group 3 — Difficulty

**Q6. What difficulty target are you aiming for?**
Options: Easy / Standard / Deadly.
*(Required. Used by: monster-scaling.md threat level, encounter-budget.md
total WP/HP pressure.)*

---

## Group 4 — Tone & Genre

**Q7. Where on the tone dial does this session sit?**
Examples: grim / gritty, darkly comic, heroic, folk-horror, weird, tragic.
Mix freely: "grim but with moments of dark humor."
*(Additional. Used by: opening hook flavor, NPC voice, handout language,
audit tone-adherence check.)*

**Q8. Are there genre conventions you want to lean into or avoid?**
Examples: "heist structure," "no undead," "dungeon crawl," "wilderness journey."
*(Additional. Used by: scene type selection, monster selection.)*

---

## Group 5 — Setting & Continuity

**Q9. Where does this session take place?**
Region, settlement, landmark, or just a vibe ("deep forest," "decaying city").
*(Additional. Used by: scene descriptions, handouts, NPC factions.)*

**Q10. Which factions or power groups are active here?**
Name them and give one-line goals if you know them.
*(Additional. Used by: NPC affiliations, encounter faction allegiances,
political complications.)*

**Q11. What continuity hooks carry over from prior sessions?**
Unresolved threads, promises made, enemies left alive, items carried.
*(Additional. Used by: opening hook options, NPC motivations, possible
sidetracks.)*

---

## Group 6 — Themes & Content Limits

**Q12. What themes do you want the session to explore?**
Examples: betrayal, redemption, cost of power, survival, belonging.
*(Additional. Used by: NPC secrets, climax shape, audit tone check.)*

**Q13. What are your Lines — content that must never appear?**
Hard limits. The skill will not write these, ever.
*(Additional. Used by: audit lines/veils adherence check, scene content.)*

**Q14. What are your Veils — content that happens off-screen or implied?**
Fade-to-black topics. The skill writes "this happens" without detail.
*(Additional. Used by: same as Lines, softer enforcement.)*
