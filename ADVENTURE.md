# THE WATCHTOWER OF SORGENFELL
### A Dragonbane Adventure for 3–5 Characters

---

## OVERVIEW

**Premise:** A goblin warband has seized an ancient watchtower and taken a hostage. A shaman inside holds a priceless relic from the Dragon Empire — but the real danger sleeps in the vault below, and the real secret is why the dead don't rest.

**Tone:** Mirth and mayhem. The goblins are not mindless brutes — they're opportunists. The wights are tragic, not merely evil. And the dragon spirit at the top of the tower has a sense of ceremony bordering on the theatrical.

**Length:** One session (3–4 hours). Suitable for new players and veterans alike.

**Reward:** ~40 gold, 150 silver, two masterwork weapons, an ancient grimoire, and one powerful magic ring.

---

## BACKGROUND

During the age of the Dragon Empire, the tower of Sorgenfell stood watch over the trade roads of the northern hills. Its garrison — twelve dragon knights sworn to the Empire — held the pass for three hundred years.

When the Empire fell to demonic corruption, the knights were betrayed by their own servants, poisoned at their evening meal and sealed inside their own vault while demons laughed above. Denied proper burial and the rites owed to warriors of the Dragon Empire, they rose as wights — cursed to guard the vault they never chose to leave.

For centuries, travelers avoided the ruin. The locals of Varenholm remember vague warnings from their grandparents. Nobody went up there. Nobody came back.

Three weeks ago, a goblin shaman named **Krix Bonewhistle** heard the old warnings and decided they meant treasure. She was right. Her warband excavated the sealed vault door, and in the process woke something. So far, the wights have not broken through — the vault door holds from the inside as well as the outside — but the groaning at night is getting louder.

Krix has found the **Commander's Signet**, a dragon-era ring of considerable magical power, among debris in the anteroom. She knows it's valuable. She does not yet know it is also the key to laying the wights to rest.

Meanwhile, a local farmer's daughter named **Lena Forster** stumbled onto the goblin camp while looking for a lost sheep. Krix had her confined rather than killed — Lena is useful insurance against interference from Varenholm.

Now three factions are converging on the little market town, all wanting the signet. And winter is coming.

---

## VARENHOLM

A prosperous market town of about four hundred souls, built around a mill and a crossroads inn. The autumn harvest is in, and the town is normally full of merchants. This year, however, a shadow hangs over the market.

### Atmosphere

When the adventurers arrive, read or paraphrase:

> *The smell of fresh bread and horse dung mingles on the morning air as you ride into Varenholm. The market square is busy — too busy, perhaps, for the season. Merchants are clustered around the well, speaking in low voices. A farmer's wife is crying near the blacksmith's. The town reeve — a heavyset man with a gold chain of office — spots you and waves you over with the relief of a drowning man spotting a rope.*

### Key NPCs in Town

---

#### ALDRIC VARENHOLT — Town Reeve
*A worried man wearing too much gold chain.*

Aldric is not corrupt; he's simply out of his depth. He manages the market taxes and keeps the peace when "the peace" means settling disputes about fence lines. Goblins in an ancient haunted tower are not in his experience.

**What he wants:** His town's grain supply secured before winter, and Lena Forster brought home.

**What he offers:** 30 gold for clearing the tower and returning Lena safely. He can also authorize the adventurers to negotiate on behalf of Varenholm — useful if they want to deal with the goblin factions.

**What he knows:** The tower has been "haunted forever." People used to disappear up there. A goblin was seen in the market three days ago trying to sell what looked like a very old sword.

**Personality:** Flustered, grateful, prone to repetition when nervous. Will promise things he cannot quite deliver (lodging at the inn, the mayor's gratitude) alongside things he can (the 30 gold).

> *"I know it sounds like a lot to ask. And I know the tower has a reputation. But Lena's father — old Bertram — he can't sleep, won't eat. If you could just bring her back, and maybe, ah, discourage the goblins from staying? I'd be very grateful. Very grateful indeed."*

---

#### MIRA CALLOWHIDE — Scholar from the Capital
*A small woman with ink-stained fingers and three too many satchels.*

Mira is a genuine academic, a member of the Imperial Archive, who has been tracking rumors of Dragon Empire relics for two years. She arrived yesterday and is already driving the innkeeper mad with questions about local history.

**What she wants:** The Commander's Signet, for academic study and documentation. She will pay 20 gold for it, or help the adventurers in exchange for access to study it first. She is not a combatant but is very smart.

**What she knows:** The Commander's Signet is described in three separate pre-Collapse texts as a ring bearing a dragon rampant in amber. It was traditionally used by garrison commanders to invoke the authority of the dragon lords — supposedly it allows the wearer to speak with dragon spirits. She suspects the wights' existence is tied to the ring's presence in the vault.

**What she can offer:** If the adventurers befriend her, she will tell them that the wights should be placeable to rest if the Signet is presented to them with the correct words of the Dragon Empire's funeral rite (she has it written down). She also knows that the goblins' shaman is named Krix and has a reputation for pragmatism over violence among goblin traders in the region.

**Personality:** Enthusiastic, talks too fast, has strong opinions about historical accuracy. Will correct people about the Dragon Empire mid-combat if given half a chance.

> *"The key thing that most people misunderstand about the wights — assuming they are wights, which all indicators suggest — is that they aren't angry. They're waiting. There's a difference. Anger you fight. Waiting you solve."*

---

#### THOG IRONGRIP — Orc Envoy
*A large orc in road-worn traveling clothes, drinking quietly in the corner.*

Thog represents Warchief Mordag, who has heard about the signet through goblin trade networks. Mordag wants it because a ring that can speak to dragon spirits would lend enormous spiritual authority to his claim over the northern hill tribes.

**What he wants:** To buy the signet from whoever ends up with it, for 50 gold. He's authorized to go to 80 if pressed (BARTERING roll to find this out; a dragon result gets him to offer 80 outright and throw in a mastercraft axe).

**What he offers:** Gold, and a letter of safe passage through Mordag's territory (worth knowing if the campaign continues).

**What he knows:** Nothing useful about the tower itself. He knows Krix Bonewhistle by reputation — "smart, greedy, not suicidal" — and can give the adventurers her name if they don't already have it.

**Personality:** Quiet, watchful, direct when he speaks. Does not insult, does not bluster. Has a dry sense of humor. Will not betray his employer but will not lie to the adventurers either.

> *"I am not here to cause trouble. Mordag pays well and keeps his word. So do I. If the ring comes up for sale, I am a buyer. If it does not, I go home. Simple."*

### Rumors in Town

The adventurers can gather information by talking to locals. Each successful AWARENESS or PERSUASION roll (depending on approach) yields one rumor, in order:

| # | Rumor | Truth |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1 | "Goblins in the market last week. One of them was selling an old coin — dragon's head on one side." | True. Krix sent scouts to assess buyers. |
| 2 | "The tower was sealed three centuries ago by a hedge-witch who said something dreadful was inside." | Partly true. The vault was sealed after the wights rose. The hedge-witch was actually a traveling animist mage. |
| 3 | "My grandfather said if you hear screaming from the tower at night, it means someone opened the vault." | Mostly false. The sounds are the wights hammering on the vault door, which they started doing after Krix arrived. |
| 4 | "There are twelve wights in that tower. My grandfather said twelve. Never one more, never one less." | Probably true. Twelve dragon knights were buried there. |
| 5 | "The goblin shaman is Krix Bonewhistle — I know that name. She sold enchanted boots to a merchant in Fordhaven. Sharp operator." | True. Mira can confirm this. |
| 6 | "Lena went up there three days ago. She's not stupid — if she's alive, she's biding her time." | True. Lena is unharmed and thinking clearly. |

## THE ROAD TO SORGENFELL

The tower is a 2-hour walk from Varenholm, up a winding track through beech woods and rocky outcroppings. The road is overgrown but passable.

### Travel Encounter: The Goblin Patrol

About halfway to the tower, the adventurers encounter a goblin patrol — two **Goblin Scouts** and a **Goblin Warrior** returning from a foraging run with a sack of stolen root vegetables.

The goblins spot the adventurers at the same moment the adventurers spot them. Nobody has to fight, but nobody has to be friendly either.

**If the adventurers are hostile:** The goblins scatter — they are not looking to die for Krix's sake. The warrior holds while the scouts run. The warrior surrenders if reduced to 3 HP or fewer.

**If the adventurers talk:** The warrior (who speaks the common tongue with heavy accent) can be persuaded (PERSUASION roll) to carry a message to Krix, or even to escort the party as "guests" to avoid a worse fight. He refuses to betray Krix outright, but he makes it very clear that the vault door is making noises and he would personally like to be elsewhere by sundown.

**Questioning a captured goblin** with a successful PERSUASION or BLUFFING roll reveals:
- Krix has fourteen goblins total (four warriors, two scouts, eight camp followers/laborers)
- The vault door is iron-bound oak. It's been groaning for two nights. Nobody opens it.
- Lena is in the upper chamber. She's fine. She beat a goblin at cards yesterday and won half his rations.
- Krix knows the ring is special. She doesn't know what it does.

### Optional Encounter: Mira on the Road

If the adventurers befriended Mira and she knows they are heading to the tower, she catches up to them on the road, slightly out of breath, carrying an extra satchel.

> *"I know you didn't invite me. I'm coming anyway. I promise to stay out of the fighting. I am, however, very good at reading inscriptions quickly while people are hitting each other."*

Mira functions as a helpful NPC if present. She provides a boon to any MYTHS & LEGENDS or LANGUAGES roll related to the Dragon Empire. She will not fight but will help with first aid (HEALING 10) and can use the wight-laying-to-rest ritual (see Area 3).

## SORGENFELL TOWER

The tower is a squat, round structure of gray stone, about fifteen meters tall, built on a rocky outcropping overlooking the valley below. A newer wooden outbuilding has been added by the goblins against the south wall. Arrow loops line the upper levels. The iron door at the base is propped open with a log.

The smell of goblin cookfire and something older — damp stone, old iron, and something faintly metallic — hangs in the air.

> *The tower rises from the hilltop like a broken tooth. Smoke curls from a gap in the wooden shed built against its base. Goblins move about the courtyard — more than you expected, though most look like laborers rather than warriors. Someone has painted a crude mural on the outer wall depicting what might be goblins celebrating, or possibly dancing, above a pile of what might be coins. The artistic interpretation is generous.*

### Getting In

**Direct assault:** The four warriors and two scouts are aware and alert. A frontal assault is winnable but costly (see combat stats above and below). The camp followers scatter.

**Stealth approach:** Two SNEAKING rolls (with banes due to the open terrain approaching the tower) get the adventurers close. From there, options open up.

**The messenger gambit:** If the adventurers sent word ahead via the goblin patrol, Krix will meet them at the door — wary, but curious. She appreciates audacity.

**Bribery/negotiation at the gate:** The gate goblin (a warrior named Skritch) is not clever but he is greedy. 3 silver buys a message to Krix. 5 silver gets the adventurers escorted inside as guests.

---

### AREA 1: THE GROUND FLOOR

*The interior of the tower's ground floor is crowded with goblin activity — sleeping pallets, a cook fire in the center vented through a cracked chimney, weapon racks improvised from old timber, and a spiral stone stair leading up.*

**Layout:**
- The ground floor is roughly 8 meters across. The fire is central.
- Four goblin warriors and two scouts occupy this floor (unless already encountered on the road).
- A locked trapdoor in the floor leads to Area 3 (the vault).
- A stone stair climbs to Area 2.

**The trapdoor** is padlocked with a new iron padlock (Krix bought it in Varenholm). It also has a heavy beam across it — improvised from an old table leg. Even if picked, the beam must be lifted (STR roll or two people cooperating). Faint sounds of metal on stone can be heard from below. **Any goblin who notices the adventurers examining the trapdoor will become agitated and call for Krix.**

**Improvised weapons on the ground floor:**
Roll D3 or select:
1. *Cookfire coals* — Throw as action; target within 4 m takes D6 fire damage and must make EVADE or catch fire (D4 fire damage per round until extinguished).
2. *Unsecured weapon rack* — Topple it as action; all creatures in a 2m cone must make EVADE or be Dazed.
3. *Barrel of grain dust* — Kick it open as action; the cloud gives all combatants in a 4m sphere a bane on ranged attacks for one round.

---

### AREA 2: THE UPPER CHAMBER

*The upper floor is Krix's quarters — cleaner than below, lit by a lantern, and decorated with maps, notes in goblin script, and a neat arrangement of excavated artifacts on a cloth-covered table. A locked iron chest sits in the corner. A narrow ladder leads to the tower top.*

**A young human woman — Lena Forster — sits on a cot, looking tired but uninjured. She is playing cards against herself. She looks up when the adventurers enter.**

#### KRIX BONEWHISTLE — Goblin Shaman

**Krix is not suicidal.** She fights only if cornered with no alternatives. If reduced to 4 HP or fewer, she calls for parley.

---

#### Negotiating with Krix

Krix is in a difficult position. She knows the ring is valuable but she doesn't know to whom, and the noises from below are making her nervous. She will negotiate if approached without aggression.

**Opening position:** She wants 80 gold for the ring, safe passage out of the tower with her warband, and a promise that whatever is in the vault stays in the vault.

**What can move her:**
- Information about what the ring actually does (a successful PERSUASION roll opens this).
- An offer that gets her warband out before the vault door gives way (she is genuinely scared of what's below).
- If Mira is present, Mira can make a direct appeal to goblin mercantile pragmatism: "You can't sell it if you're dead. Tell me what you've found. I can help."
- If the adventurers mention that they can lay the wights to rest, Krix drops her price to 30 gold and offers to help search the vault once the threat is neutralized.

**If she deals:** Krix hands over the Commander's Signet from around her own neck (she's been wearing it "for protection"), orders her warband to pack up and leave, and opens the trapdoor. She does not go down, but she won't stop the adventurers either.

**Lena Forster:** Lena has been treated reasonably well — fed, not harmed, kept bored. If the adventurers rescue her in a fight, she grabs a knife from a goblin's belt and covers the door while they deal with Krix. If negotiations go well, Krix releases her voluntarily as part of the deal.

**Lena knows:**
- The vault sounds started two nights ago — rhythmic thumping, always in groups of three.
- She saw Krix studying the ring very carefully and muttering to herself. Krix looked frightened when the thumping started.
- There is a second door up the ladder to the tower top. Krix goes up there alone sometimes and stands very still.

---

#### Krix's Chest

The iron chest (locked, SLEIGHT OF HAND to pick or break it open with STR roll) contains:
- 3D6 × 5 silver (goblin savings and market proceeds)
- A rolled map showing the vault layout as best Krix could sketch it (useful for Area 3)
- A journal in goblin script (Mira or LANGUAGES to read): Krix's notes on the ring, which include the passage "the ring hums when I hold it near the vault door. It hums differently at the top of the tower. I think something up there hears it."

---

### AREA 3: THE VAULT

*Down the trapdoor and a short stone stair, a low passage leads to an iron door, slightly ajar — the beam that originally barred it has been broken outward from the inside.*

The vault is a rectangular chamber, 10 meters by 8 meters, with vaulted stone ceilings 3 meters high. Twelve stone sarcophagi are arranged in two rows. Six of them are open, their lids lying cracked on the floor. Six remain sealed.

**The wights are already free from their coffins.** They stand in the vault, still and silent, facing the iron door. When the adventurers enter:

> *They are tall, in corroded armor that might once have been magnificent. Dragon motifs are still faintly visible in the metalwork. They hold ancient longswords that gleam despite the centuries of rust around everything else. None of them move. None of them speak. But twelve pairs of eyes — dim, cold, and patient — turn toward you.*

**The wights do not immediately attack.** They are waiting for something. Specifically, they are waiting for the Commander's Signet.

**If an adventurer is wearing or holding the Signet:** The wights hold their position. The holder of the Signet feels a vibration and hears, just barely, a name: *Karathos*. This is the name of their former commander. Speaking this name aloud causes all twelve wights to go still, then slowly lower their weapons.

**If nobody has the Signet, or if the adventurers attack:** The wights fight. All twelve. This is a very difficult fight — the GM should consider having non-hostile wights remain passive unless attacked directly, making the encounter survivable but terrifying.

**Performing the funeral rite (if Mira or a MYTHS & LEGENDS roll revealed it):** With the Signet in hand, speaking the words of the Dragon Empire's burial rite (takes one stretch, counts as a ritual) causes each wight to kneel and dissolve into ash, leaving only their armor and weapons. The room goes quiet.

---

#### WIGHT (Dragon Knight)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Movement** | 8 |
| **HP** | 16 |
| **Armor** | 3 (corroded plate, bludgeoning damage gets +2 due to brittleness — treat as armor 1 vs bludgeoning) |

**Weapons**
- *Longsword.* Skill 14. Damage 2D8, piercing/slashing. Can parry.

**Fear Attack:** On first contact with a wight, all characters within 10 meters must make a WIL roll or consult the fear table.

**Special: Undying.** When a wight reaches 0 HP, it makes no death rolls — instead it collapses and rises again on its next turn with D6 HP restored, unless destroyed by the BANISH or PURGE spells, or by the Commander's Signet ritual.

**Special: Cold Presence.** Each round spent adjacent to a wight causes the loss of 1 WP (in addition to any other effects).

**Immune to:** Fear, poison, sleeping poison, disease.

**Note:** Only two of the twelve wights engage unless attacked. The GM should use this as an investigation and roleplay challenge, not a slaughterhouse.

---

#### The Vault's Contents

After the wights are laid to rest (or after a very messy fight), the vault contains:

**On the wights** (if they've been dissolved by the rite, their gear remains):
- 6 sets of corroded plate armor (armor rating 1 now, but potentially restorable by a skilled Artisan using CRAFTING)
- 12 ancient longswords — 2 of them are **Mastercrafted** (reduced STR requirement, increased durability)
- Ancient dragon-motif brooches worth 5 gold each to a collector (there are twelve)

**In a sealed alcove at the back of the vault (SPOT HIDDEN to find):**
- A locked iron chest containing 40 gold in old Dragon Empire coins, still valid currency
- A grimoire belonging to the garrison's mage: contains LIGHTNING FLASH (Animism) and TREAT WOUND (Animism) at rank 1. A non-animist mage needs LANGUAGES to read it.

### AREA 4 (OPTIONAL): THE TOWER TOP

The ladder from Area 2 leads to a trapdoor and the open top of the tower. The stonework here is worn but intact. On a clear day, the valley below and the road to Varenholm are visible.

This encounter only triggers if:
- An adventurer wears the Commander's Signet to the top, or
- The wights have been successfully laid to rest.

When the conditions are met:

> *As you emerge onto the tower top, the wind picks up — and then something else comes with it. A shape forms in the air above the battlements: coils of translucent smoke that catch the low sunlight in amber and gold. It is enormous. It is serpentine. It regards you with eyes like ember-lit coins and says, in a voice like a mountain shifting:*
>
> *"The knights are at rest. The debt is paid. Keep the signet, if you wish. Or give it back to the earth. Either way — I was here before this tower, and I will be here after it falls. But you have done a thing worth remembering."*
>
> *Then it is gone. The wind drops. The valley is very quiet.*

This is **Skythraax**, an ancient dragon, diminished but not gone. She will not fight. She will answer three questions if the adventurers have treated her knights with respect. She knows a great deal about Dragon Empire history. The GM should decide exactly what she reveals based on the campaign's needs.

**If asked about the Commander's Signet:** "It is bound to this place. If you take it far, it will go dim. If you return it, it will stay bright. I do not command — I only observe."

**If asked about the wights:** "They were good soldiers who died badly. Now they are at peace. I am grateful."

**If asked what she wants:** A long pause. "Nothing from you. You have already given me what I wanted."

## AFTERMATH & REWARDS

### Returning to Varenholm

Aldric is relieved and effusively grateful. He pays the 30 gold without quibbling. If Lena is returned safely, Bertram Forster brings the adventurers a wheel of cheese and a hug.

**Other resolutions:**

| Outcome | Additional Reward |
|---------|-----------------|
| Sold the Signet to Mira | +20 gold; Mira promises to credit the adventurers in her paper |
| Sold the Signet to Thog | +50–80 gold; letter of safe passage from Warchief Mordag |
| Kept the Signet | Ring stays functional near Sorgenfell; grows dim beyond ~10 km |
| Returned the Signet to the tower top | Skythraax's blessing — once, one member can re-roll any roll at the tower's bearing |
| Laid all twelve wights to rest | Each PC gains an advancement mark as well as normal session rewards |

### The Commander's Signet

If the adventurers keep it near Sorgenfell (within a day's ride), the ring has the following properties:

- Acts as a PROTECTOR spell at power level 2 permanently (armor against magic for the wearer).
- The wearer can sense undead within 20 meters (a persistent cool vibration).
- Costs 0 WP to use the protection — it runs on old dragonfire.

The signet grows dim and loses its power if taken more than ~80 km from the tower.

## EXPERIENCE & ADVANCEMENT

At the end of the session, ask the standard Dragonbane advancement questions:

- Did you participate? *Yes, obviously.*
- Did you explore a new location? *Yes — Sorgenfell Tower (and possibly the vault).*
- Did you defeat a dangerous adversary? *Yes — wights, or Krix, or both.*
- Did you overcome an obstacle without force? *Yes, if they negotiated with Krix or laid the wights to rest through roleplay.*
- Did you give in to your weakness? *(Optional — award if applicable.)*

A full session should yield 3–4 advancement marks for most characters.

## GM NOTES

### On Tone

This adventure can be played dead seriously or with considerable humor. The goblins are not evil; they are employees of Krix who would rather be paid than fight. Krix herself is a pragmatic professional who made a poor choice about which ruin to loot. Playing her as competent and reasonable — rather than cackling and villainous — rewards players who try to talk first.

The wights are the emotional core of the adventure. Twelve loyal soldiers, killed by betrayal, unable to rest for centuries. If the adventurers can feel sympathy for them rather than just combat-roll them into dust, the ending lands harder.

Skythraax is the punctuation on the adventure's theme: that some things worth doing are not about gold.

### On the Vault Fight

If the players attack the wights directly without the Signet, this is a brutal encounter. Use it to signal genuine danger — have the wights focus on one character at a time, making death rolls likely — but also give the players opportunities to use the Signet (maybe someone finds it in the chest upstairs, or Krix tosses it down the trapdoor to help, if relations are good). The Undying trait is there to teach the lesson that fighting is not always the path.

### Scaling the Adventure

**Easier:** Remove the Undying trait. Reduce wight HP to 10. Give Krix only one combat spell.

**Harder:** All twelve wights engage immediately. Add a **Harpy** nesting on the tower top (Skythraax encounter does not occur). Give Krix the RESURRECTION spell already learned.

### Campaign Hooks

- Warchief Mordag is unhappy if the signet wasn't delivered to Thog. His scouts may be interested in the adventurers.
- Mira's paper about the Dragon Empire vault causes a sensation in the capital. A rival scholar disputes her findings — and claims the adventurers are thieves.
- The six sealed sarcophagi in the vault were never opened. What's in them? (The GM decides — dragon knight relics, old secrets, or something darker brought back by the garrison in the Empire's final days.)
- Skythraax's name is in the old records. Other people — not all of them friendly — may come looking for her.

### Key Rules Reminders

- **Dragon roll (1):** Crit — choose double damage dice, free second attack, or ignore armor (piercing only)
- **Demon roll (20):** Fumble — miss, roll D6 for extra mishap, cannot push
- **Parrying:** Declare before attacker rolls damage; uses your turn; dragon parry = free counterattack
- **Dodging:** Declare before attacker rolls damage; uses your turn; success = move 2m free
- **Death:** At 0 HP — three death rolls each turn; 3 success = recover D6 HP; 3 fail = dead
- **Stretch rest:** D6 HP + D6 WP + 1 condition healed (interrupted = nothing)
- **Shift rest:** Full HP/WP + all conditions (must be safe, uninterrupted)
